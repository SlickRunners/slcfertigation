Frequently Asked Questions

What is fertigation?

Fertigation is the delivery of liquid fertilizer directly through an irrigation system so a lawn is fed automatically every time it waters. It's the same technology golf courses and commercial groundskeepers have used for decades, now installed on residential sprinkler systems.

How long does installation take?

Installation is completed in one day. The system connects to your existing irrigation lines — no new lines or major excavation required — and your first feeding happens less than 24 hours after install.

How much does fertigation cost?

Installation is a one-time investment of $3,997, which includes the fertigation system, a smart WiFi controller upgrade, a full sprinkler nozzle retrofit, and a full first year of fertilizer refills and maintenance. After year one, service continues at $100/month.

Is there a guarantee?

Yes. Mr. Fertigation offers a 90-Day Healthy Lawn Guarantee: if you don't see visible improvement in your lawn within 90 days of installation, we return to diagnose and resolve the issue at no charge.

Where does Mr. Fertigation provide service?

We serve residential properties in Salt Lake City and Park City, Utah.

Does fertigation save water?

Yes. Because fertigation is paired with a smart controller and precision rotary nozzles, the system reduces water waste and dry spots while using your existing irrigation schedule more efficiently — resulting in a greener lawn with less water than hand-fertilizing and standard sprinkler heads.
