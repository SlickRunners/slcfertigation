The Dirt on Dirt

Ideal Soil vs. Typical Utah Soil

Healthy soil isn't just "dirt" — it's a precise mix of minerals, air, and organic matter. Most Wasatch Front yards sit on heavy clay left behind by ancient Lake Bonneville, which behaves nothing like the soil your grass actually wants.

  • ✕ High pH (often 7.5–8.5) locks up nutrients before roots can use them

  • ✓ Balanced pH (6.2–7.0) keeps nutrients available to roots
  • ✓ Holds moisture like a sponge — releasing it slowly between waterings
Why It Matters

Healthy Soil Means a Smaller Water Bill

Clay-heavy soil sheds water before roots can use it, so sprinklers have to run longer and more often to keep grass alive. Soil built to hold moisture needs far less water to stay green — here's what that looks like over a typical Utah summer.
